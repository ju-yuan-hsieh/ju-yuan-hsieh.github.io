謝如媛

國立政治大學法學院 教授

謝如媛教授，主要研究領域為刑事政策，特別是少年法、修復式司法、犯罪人處遇，講授科目有刑事實體法、刑事政策、少年法、犯罪學與日文法學名著選讀相關課程，同時於台日均有學術作品發表並與實務工作者交流。

Ju-Yuan Hsieh

Professor, College of Law, National Chengchi University

Professor Ju-Yuan Hsieh specializes in criminal policy, with particular focus on juvenile law, restorative justice, and offender treatment. Her teaching portfolio includes substantive criminal law, criminal policy, juvenile law, criminology, and selected readings in Japanese legal literature. She has published widely across Taiwan and Japan, actively engaging in dialogue with legal practitioners.
